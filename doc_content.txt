Product Definition and Non-Negotiable ScopePOLARIS is a remote operations command portal for India’s Antarctic research infrastructure. The prototype must feel like a real mission-control system rather than a generic admin dashboard. The experience is built around four routes and four distinct 3D experiences.1.1 Final Route StructureRoutePagePurpose3D ExperiencePrimary Action/India Antarctic Mission OverviewNational network overview3D Antarctica command globe/terrainEnter command center or a station/commandCommand ControlCross-station remote mission control3D Antarctic operations command tableCompare, monitor, respond/maitriMaitri — Station 01Station-specific operationsInteractive Maitri digital twinInspect systems and telemetry/bharatiBharati — Station 02Station-specific operationsInteractive Bharati digital twinInspect systems and telemetry1.2 Core Experience RulesEvery page must have a clear operational purpose; do not reuse the same dashboard with a different title.3D is functional, not decorative. Clicking a 3D station, zone, vehicle, or subsystem must open relevant operational information.The interface must communicate status in under five seconds: normal, warning, critical, offline, maintenance, unknown.The portal must work with simulated telemetry so the demo appears live without requiring Antarctic hardware.The system must demonstrate degraded-connectivity behavior and local autonomous operation.Animations should feel cinematic but fast: most transitions 180–450 ms; route fly-ins approximately 0.8–1.5 s.No uncontrolled feature sprawl. Finish the four-page experience before adding optional modules.2.
